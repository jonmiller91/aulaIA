### Design System Overview

## Concept and Style A premium digital product system focused on clarity, precision, and confidence. The visual language emphasizes generous whitespace, restrained aesthetics, and strong information hierarchy, allowing photography and product content to become the primary visual assets. Every interface element is intentionally reduced to its essential form while maintaining a refined, luxury-oriented experience. Hierarchy Primary: Black Secondary: Dark gray Tertiary: Mid gray Disabled: Light gray Interactive states rely on: Shape Motion Contrast rather than color alone. ### Accessibility Guidelines Minimum **WCAG** AA compliance Large interaction targets (≥44px) Clear focus indicators Consistent spacing improves scanability Status never communicated by color alone Preserve semantic typography hierarchy ### Design Principles Clarity over decoration Every component serves a functional purpose. Visual reduction enhances usability rather than simplifying aesthetics alone. Product-first composition The interface frames and supports content instead of competing with it. Photography, media, and product information remain the primary visual focus. Structural consistency Spacing, alignment, sizing, and rhythm form the foundation of the visual language, creating predictability and reducing cognitive load. Controlled emphasis Attention is directed through hierarchy, whitespace, typography, and scale—not through excessive color, animation, or ornamentation. Quiet luxury The experience communicates quality through restraint, precision, craftsmanship, and meticulous attention to proportion rather than overt visual richness.
